QUESTION
  D2 (../tp_share_sweep) established that end-to-end INC gain is critical-path
  structural (sits ABOVE the byte-share Amdahl ceiling for pipeline-parallel
  configs), measured on a 2-layer/seq-128 TOY block.  [2026-07-14 caveat: the
  re-measured plain-C3 toy point is now NEGATIVE under the placeholder compute
  model -- see VERDICT and ../tp_share_sweep.]  This check scales the C3
  anchor's GEOMETRY only -- Llama3 block num_layers 2->8, seq_len 128->512,
  hidden_size 4096 and batch 1 unchanged -- keeping C3's parallelism (TP4/DP2/
  PP2, 16 ranks, plain Megatron TP), the compute cost model, engine, topos and
  flags identical.  A pure geometry-scaling check on the EXISTING generator +
  engine.  Primary config ran; no fallback needed.

MEASUREMENT FIX (verified in pcm-sdk source 2026-07-13, fixed 2026-07-14)
  The pcm-sdk engine takes the per-GPU scale-up NIC injection rate from
  -intranode_linkspeed (Mbps), NOT from the .topo file (which sets only the
  fabric pipes).  The flag was never passed in the 2026-07-13 runs, so it
  defaulted to COPY_ENG = 200,000 Mbps = 200 Gbps: UecNIC::startSending held
  the port 166 ns per 4,150 B frame = 24.61 payload-B/ns = 5.0% of the
  fabric's realised 492.3 B/ns.  Every p2p arm whose per-send block exceeded
  one frame (4,086 B payload) was silently NIC-capped (this trace's decomposed
  TP sends are 1-2 MiB); the ACK-less INC datapath bypasses the NIC pacer and
  was NEVER capped -- the INC makespan reproduces BYTE-IDENTICALLY with the
  flag.  Re-run 2026-07-14 with -intranode_linkspeed 3600000: the NIC paces at
  9.22 ns/frame = 443.1 payload-B/ns (Mbps arithmetic; the fabric pipes'
  2 ps/B quantisation realises 492.3 B/ns).

VERDICT  (rewritten 2026-07-14; the 2026-07-13 verdict overstated)
  At the fixed NIC the 8-layer/seq-512 C3 point still shows a solid positive
  end-to-end gain (+9.13%) sitting ~2.7x above the byte-share Amdahl ceiling,
  so the critical-path-structural character of the gain survives at the
  realistic block geometry.  But the 2-layer C3 toy anchor FLIPS NEGATIVE
  (-4.6453%): the fixed-NIC plain-C3 baseline (219,352,385 ns) is FASTER than
  the INC arm (229,541,927 ns, unchanged).  This is not the collective
  datapath (per-op INC durations are 3.5-9 us; the INC arm's last collective
  completes at 148.8 ms of its 229.5 ms makespan, and the ~81 ms coll-free
  tail differs structurally from the baseline's schedule) -- a schedule/
  congestion-structure effect, mechanism under investigation.  Under the
  calibrated H100 roofline compute model the same 2-layer config gives
  +8.8133% (see computemodel/), consistent with this 8-layer +9.1321% -- the
  sign flip is specific to the near-free placeholder compute model.  So the
  2026-07-13 chain "D2 holds and is reinforced via the 2L->8L comparison" no
  longer stands as stated; the honest statement is: the positive, above-
  ceiling gain holds at the realistic geometry (and at 2 layers under the
  calibrated compute model), while the 2-layer near-free-compute toy point is
  negative.
