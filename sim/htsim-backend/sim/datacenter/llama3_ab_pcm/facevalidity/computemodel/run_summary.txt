  COMPUTE_MODEL (uncommitted Fix-B prototype; NOT reverted, NOT modified here).
    - unset/"placeholder": original behaviour, byte-for-byte.  Effectively
      compute ~1 PFLOP/s (== flops//1e6 consumed as ns) and memory ~1 PB/s

    - "h100": explicit H100-SXM roofline, duration = max(compute, memory) ns,
      compute = flops/(989e12*0.45)*1e9, memory = (mem*2)/(3.35e12*0.70)*1e9.

  Pipeline/flags/groups conversion identical to the face-validity run.  Default
  config IS the C3 anchor, so build_full_training was NOT edited.  Same 16

  render {placebo,h100} x {baseline,inc}, node-local groups (rank%4), txt2bin,

MEASUREMENT FIX (verified in pcm-sdk source 2026-07-13, fixed 2026-07-14)
  The engine takes the per-GPU scale-up NIC injection rate from
  -intranode_linkspeed (Mbps), NOT from the .topo file (fabric pipes only).
  The 2026-07-13 runs never passed it, so the NIC defaulted to COPY_ENG =
  200,000 Mbps = 200 Gbps (24.61 payload-B/ns = 5.0% of the fabric's realised
  492.3 B/ns) and every p2p arm with per-send blocks over one 4,086 B-payload
  frame was silently NIC-capped; the ACK-less INC datapath bypasses the NIC
  pacer and was NEVER capped (both INC makespans reproduce byte-identically
  with the flag).  All four arms re-run 2026-07-14 with
  -intranode_linkspeed 4000000 appended to the archived invocations
  (NIC 9.22 ns/frame = 443.1 payload-B/ns Mbps arithmetic; the fabric pipes'
  2 ps/B quantisation realises 492.3 B/ns).  Zero drops and zero lossless-
  headroom warnings in every re-run.

WHY THE MODELS NOW DISAGREE IN SIGN (rewritten 2026-07-14; the old "gain rises
because comm-bound makespan barely moves" story was measured on a capped NIC)
  * PLACEBO (near-free compute): the fixed-NIC baseline (228,360,322 ns) is
    FASTER than the INC arm (229,541,927 ns) -> gain -0.5174%.  This is NOT
    the collective datapath: per-op INC durations are 3.5-9 us, and the INC
    arm's last collective completes at 148.8 ms of its 229.5 ms makespan --
    the ~81 ms coll-free tail differs structurally from the baseline's
    schedule.  A schedule/congestion-structure effect, mechanism under
    investigation.
  * H100 (calibrated roofline): realistic per-op compute serialises the
    transformer block (norm->attn->AR->norm->mlp->AR), each TP AllReduce sits
    on a serial critical path with real compute either side, and INC saves
    21,618,358 ns end-to-end -> gain +-5.2153%.  This mechanism survives the
    NIC fix (the h100 gain was +8.2720% capped, +-5.2153% fixed).
  * Honest note: the h100 BASELINE got 1.45 ms SLOWER with the FASTER NIC
    (243,844,206 -> 212,586,583 ns) -- faster injection reshapes contention on
    the shared tiers, a congestion-structure effect, so "faster NIC => faster
    makespan" does not hold arm-by-arm.

VERDICT (rewritten 2026-07-14)
  The compute model is now DECISION-RELEVANT, not a robustness footnote: under
  the near-free placeholder model the fixed-NIC C3 gain is NEGATIVE (-0.5174%),
  while under the hardware-calibrated H100 roofline it is +-5.2153% (and the
  8-layer face-validity geometry gives +9.1321%).  The 2026-07-13 claim that
  the headline is robust to the compute model -- "positive under both, only
  the percentage moves" -- does NOT survive the NIC fix.  What does survive:
  all collectives complete with zero drops under both models, both INC
  makespans are NIC-independent, the h100-vs-placebo shift is still UP and
  larger (-4.6979 pp), and the calibrated-model gain remains positive and
  structural.  The placebo sign flip is specific to the near-free compute
  model and is under investigation as a schedule/congestion-structure effect.

RESOLVED 2026-07-15 (see tp_share_sweep/schedule_sensitivity.md): no bug.
The collective datapath is verified FASTER than recursive doubling even under a
deliberate 20 ms member skew (coll +3.6 us vs RD +14.1 us after the straggler);
both renderings pay a ~35-40x group-synchronisation amplification over their
serial TP cost; and a nanosecond-scale dependency-preserving schedule
perturbation moves BOTH arms' makespans by +-5-9% (16-28 ms) under BOTH compute
models. Every single-schedule end-to-end delta at sub-percent TP share
(-4.65% placebo, +8.81% H100, +-0.2% C1/C2/C4) is at or below that floor and is
reported as UNRESOLVED, not as a gain or loss. Perturbation-ensemble means lean
INC-positive (+2.5% placebo / +8.0% roofline, n=3-4). C5/SP-C5 (4.2x/8.4x) are
far above the floor and stand.
Consequence for this experiment: the placebo-vs-h100 SHIFT (+13.46 pp) mixes
two sub-floor point estimates; the defensible statement is that the compute
model relocates the anchor WITHIN the +-5-9% sensitivity band, not that either
point is a calibrated gain (h100 unperturbed +8.81%, h100 perturbed -1.7%).
